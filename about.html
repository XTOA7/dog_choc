About This Calculator

The Dog Chocolate Toxicity Calculator is a free, independent tool that helps dog owners estimate the theobromine dose their dog may have ingested and understand the corresponding risk level.

Why this exists

Chocolate poisoning is one of the most common pet emergencies in the UK. According to the Veterinary Poisons Information Service (VPIS), milk chocolate alone accounted for over 1,100 poisoning inquiries in the 2025 annual report. Yet the information available online is often scattered, inconsistent, or buried in clinical jargon.

This calculator distils the dose thresholds reported in peer-reviewed veterinary research and UK clinical guidance into a single, simple form. It takes the three pieces of information that UK vets ask for first — the type of chocolate, the amount eaten, and the dog's weight — and returns an estimated dose in mg/kg alongside a plain-English risk band.

Who built it

This is an independent project built by a developer with an interest in pet safety. It is not affiliated with any veterinary practice, chocolate manufacturer, or pet food company.

What it is — and isn't

This tool is for education and triage only. It does not replace professional veterinary advice. Methylxanthine content varies by brand and recipe, and individual dogs respond differently. If your dog has eaten chocolate, contact your vet or Animal PoisonLine (UK, 24/7: 01202 509000, charged service) immediately.

Sources

The dosage data and risk thresholds are based on the MSD Veterinary Manual and the peer-reviewed study by Weingart et al. (2021), corroborated by UK guidance from the Royal Veterinary College, BSAVA, and the Kennel Club. Full references are listed on the calculator page.
